# Ship

1. **Re-check before you push.** Run the project's checks (format, types, tests) and the relevant `verify-*` skill. Read `principals/prove-it-works.md`.
2. **Review your own diff** as a stranger would. Remove debug output, stray files, and unrelated edits.
3. **Stage explicit paths.** Never `git add -A` when other work may share the tree.
4. **Commit in small ordered commits** with messages in the repo's style. Failing check before fix where that applies.
5. **Open the pull request** with: what and why in two or three lines, how it was verified (paste the proof), and what a reviewer should look at.
6. **Do not merge** unless the human asked you to.

**Reply:** the PR link and the verification summary.
